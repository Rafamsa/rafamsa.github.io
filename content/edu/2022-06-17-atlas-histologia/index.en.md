The "Interactive Histology Atlas" project, carried out by UNIFAL-MG, is an innovative educational resource that offers a visual, interactive approach to studying histology, the science that investigates biological tissues.

It was developed by a team of researchers from the Federal University of Alfenas (UNIFAL-MG) and aims to make histological concepts easier to understand through detailed images, clear descriptions and interactive features.

The Atlas drawings were made by me, Rafael Martins, under the name of my artist profile, R.YO, under the supervision of professors Dr. Manuella Carvalho da Costa and Dr. Deila Rosély Carneiro.

To access the Atlas, click [here](https://www.unifal-mg.edu.br/histologiainterativa/).
To access the drawings tab, with drawings made by me, click [here](https://www.unifal-mg.edu.br/histologiainterativa/videoaula/) and have fun learning about animal tissues!
