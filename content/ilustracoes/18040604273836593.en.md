"Panic"

When herds or travelers felt an inexplicable fear in the forest, they attributed it to the invisible cry of Pan.

This fear of the hidden in the forest was translated into the fear of what is hidden inside ourselves, or in life itself.

While fear is rational — it has a reason, a target — panic goes beyond logical explanation. It's like the fear of "Nothing", because the irrational has taken over the being.
