"Baliza?"

Yep, "baliza" (the Portuguese word for this prompt). Being the country boy I am, I thought "baliza" was only the driving maneuver (parallel parking), but no — it's also a device/object used to signal and mark off something or a space... like a TRAFFIC CONE.

Anyway, good night, and enjoy the song that has nothing to do with the "baliza".
