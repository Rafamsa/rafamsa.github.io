"Water Wolf"

The Lobo d'água (Water Wolf), or Afogado (the Drowned One), is a werewolf that imitates the sound of people drowning in rivers (especially children). They say its cry sounds human, and when someone jumps into the river to save it, it attacks and devours its prey.

According to the legend, he was a wicked man who, hoping for redemption, tried to save a child from drowning but died along with them. Now he's trapped in this murderous body.
