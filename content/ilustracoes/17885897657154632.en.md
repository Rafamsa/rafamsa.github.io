"Sound of Madness"

Sound of Madness, or simply the Cursed Pup, is a guardian of riparian forests, rivers and other bodies of water. 
They say that when someone destroys these environments, this giant otter pup makes that person hear a very loud waterfall inside their mind. The sound grows louder every second, and some people say they still hear it years after the animal appeared.
