"Reading Time"

That late afternoon, during reading time, I fell asleep. I dreamed of the Fungi Kingdom (the subject of my book) and of how its societies formed under a solid equality, where everyone had food to eat and shade to rest in.
However, even in deep sleep, I felt those eyes studying me as if I were the book, not the Fungi Kingdom.
