"Natural Food"

Just a black-tufted marmoset (Callithrix penicillata) feeding on a nice beetle.

Here I used a series of art styles to depict an animal from our fauna. Even though it has invaded many places it shouldn't have (I blame humans, and so should you), it's still a really cute primate.

Note: the beetle was caught by the marmoset. Don't feed wild animals: besides the risk of spreading diseases (like rabies), you can also make the animal dependent on human food.

That's it.
