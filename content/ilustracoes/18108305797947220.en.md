"Potions Class"

That day, class was a party! The pupil finally nailed the potion she had spent weeks perfecting.
— HOORAY!! — she shouted, while the potion splashed all over the room.
— Congratulations! — said the proud teacher.

To all the teachers of this country and the world (and of other worlds too), I wish you lots of strength and congratulations. But above all, thank you. Thank you for making the world (or worlds) a better place!

Happy Teachers' Day!!! (October 15 in Brazil)
