"Out of Water"

I didn't believe him when he said that, being a fish, all he could do was swim. So he came out of the water and showed me how gangly he was on dry land.

Biology pill: the Pirarucu (arapaima), our Amazonian giant, is the largest scaled freshwater fish in the world. Its scales, up to 10 cm long, are extremely tough (but, contrary to what people say, they don't resist firearms) and are used in many ways in Indigenous and riverside cultures of the Amazon. And where does its funny name come from? From two words: "pira", fish, and "urucum", red, because of its reddish scales which, during the breeding season, become more intensely colored in males.
