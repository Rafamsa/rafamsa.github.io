"Moonlight"

— Selene! — the woman cried out.
That was when the goddess of moonlight, mysticism and magic appeared on the horizon.
— What do you want, child? — Selene answered.
— Take me with you, I swear I'll be useful. — She knelt before the deity.
— You love the Sun as you love the Moon. You love the Night as you love the Day. You do not deserve to come with me.
And so Selene left, leaving the woman weeping on the hill.
