"Princess Kaguya"

One day, a bamboo cutter spotted an extremely bright light inside a bamboo stalk. When he opened it, he found a child the size of a thumb. With his wife's help, they raised this new life.
Her name would be Kaguya, and she became the most beautiful woman in the village. However, she didn't belong to Japan — she belonged to the moon!

The image above is from when Kaguya decided to visit Brazil and ran into a Sapo-cururu (cane toad), but she didn't know she was afraid of toads until that moment! 🐸
