"Onkos"

She was the strongest warrior in the Kingdom, but that wound in her chest, caused by the monster Onkos, reminded us how fragile life is.

Today is a special day: one of the strongest women I've ever known would have had her birthday today, but the creature Onkos was too strong.

October is an important month to remember this disease that affects so many people around the world: breast cancer.

Take this reminder to get your health checked, or to ask the women you love whether their exams are up to date this year. Care is also a form of fighting!

P.S.: I miss you, but I know you're in a better place ❤️
