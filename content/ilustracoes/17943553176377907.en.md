"The Anthropomorphism of Fear"

Fear knocked on my door and I decided to open it, but the creature I saw was bigger and worse than any fear I had ever felt or witnessed. So I ran, because those twisted, damaged, flexible limbs, even without touching me, tore away a part of me with every second of my escape.
