"South bird"

The South Bird is a bird in the world of One Piece that always faces south — after all, having its face turned in any other direction is a huge nuisance. In other words, it's a compass!

Note: there are also North Birds, Eastern Birds and Western Birds; the difference is the direction they face, the pattern on the beak and the headpieces.
