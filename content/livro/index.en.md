*It's not the end of the worlds, but it is the scariest part of them.*

In this story, the Nightmare, an entity from the world of dreams, chases his dream across the nine worlds of Conglomerate 742. But could his dream give nightmares even to the wisest king or the mightiest warrior?

Follow Ikelos's journey and how his actions affect characters like Colven, a murderous cowboy; Makiho, a detective with powers; and even Metus, his own father and a frightened rabbit.

Come discover this story… but you can come without Fear ("Medo").

The book is currently available in Portuguese only.
